Mentoring Documentation

This space documents the mentoring of students by Department of Linguistics faculty members and staff. At present, it contains two collections: a record of faculty mentoring and transformative student experiences, and an open teaching materials that came out of that mentored work (both accessible under Mentoring Stories).

Mentoring Archive

Faculty mentoring and student experiences

A dynamic record of how faculty-student out-of-class interactions shape the growth, character, and professional trajectories of our students — office hours, research, lab work, study abroad, and other experiential learning.    Faculty document their mentoring activities; students reflect on transformative experiences in light of the Aims of a BYU Education.
