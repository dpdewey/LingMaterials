Other places to look

Automated searches miss a lot of student internships, which are often posted only on campus systems or behind a sign-in.

  • BYU Handshake — Most employer-posted internships aimed at BYU students land here first; sign in with your BYU account and filter by Job type → Internship.
  • Your college's internship office — Ask about for-credit internships, funding, and employers who have hosted BYU students before.
  • LinkedIn (Jobs → Internship filter) — Turn on job alerts for searches like "localization intern" or "editorial intern".
  • USAJobs Pathways — Federal student internships; search with Hiring path → Students.
  • Professional associations — LSA, ACL, ACES, TESOL International and ATA list summer programs and student opportunities.

How to read this

This is a snapshot from 2026-11-05; internships open and close quickly, so always check the original ad. 41 of the 273 listings are marked check: the ad's description or job-type field says it's an internship but the title doesn't, so confirm before applying. Terms, pay, deadlines, and program fit are read automatically from the ad text and can be wrong.
