This survey asks about your opinions and personal experiences with brain drain and community development. There are no right or wrong answers — we are interested in your genuine views, including any ambivalence or conflict you may feel.

Section A asks about what you think works well in keeping talented people in rural communities. Section B asks about what you think doesn't work well or causes people to leave. Section C collects brief background information.

Your responses are anonymous and will be used only to support classroom discussion.

OPEN-ENDED QUESTIONS

Write a few words or a sentence — your honest first thoughts are more valuable than a polished answer.

OPEN QUESTION 1
In one sentence: why did you choose to work where you work now? Was it primarily a choice, or primarily a circumstance?
OPEN QUESTION 2
Think of a talented former student who left your region. What was the main reason they left? Do you think the community lost something important when they left?
OPEN QUESTION 3
What is the single most important thing your institution or regional government could do right now to make young graduates more likely to stay?
OPEN QUESTION 4
On a scale from 1 to 10 — how rooted do you personally feel in your community? What makes you feel that way?
