STUDY IT — THEN TEACH IT!

Future forms — will / going to / present progressive · ~25 minutes · You will teach this to a classmate

← L6INDEXL8 →
YOUR MISSION: In a few minutes, you will be the teacher. A classmate will learn this from YOU — your explanation, your examples, your questions. As you study, keep asking: How will I make my student understand this? The purple boxes are your teaching plan.
1BE GOING TO — PLANS & EVIDENCE~6 MIN
For intentions you decided BEFORE speaking, and for predictions with visible evidence right now.
I’m going to study medicine. (my plan)
Look at those clouds — it’s going to rain. (evidence)
🎓 PLAN IT · How will you explain this?

Write the words you will actually SAY to your student. Tip: draw or show an example first — teachers who show are remembered!

2WILL — PREDICTIONS & ON-THE-SPOT DECISIONS~6 MIN
For opinions about the future (often with probably, I think, maybe) and for decisions made at the moment of speaking — offers, promises, snap choices.
I think she’ll win. · You’ll love this film.
The phone’s ringing — I’ll get it! (decided right now)
🎓 PLAN IT · Your teaching examples

Create TWO true sentences about your own life to teach with, then one QUESTION to ask so your student discovers the pattern instead of just hearing it:

3PRESENT PROGRESSIVE — FIXED ARRANGEMENTS (+ THE TIME-CLAUSE TRAP)~5 MIN
For arrangements already set with other people (time/place agreed): I’m meeting Ana at six. And after when / before / after / as soon as / if, use PRESENT, not will.
We’re flying to Quito on Friday. (tickets bought)
I’ll call you when I arrive. ✘ when I will arrive
🎓 PLAN IT · Predict your student's mistake

Your student will probably say something like “I will call you when I will arrive” — plan a kind, clear correction:

4MINI-QUIZ — test your student!~4 MIN

Solve them yourself FIRST and note the answers small — then give them to your student at the end and check their learning.

Q1 · The doorbell! I ______ (answer) it.  my answer: ______________________
Q2 · Correct or not? “We’ll have dinner after the movie will end.” Fix it.  my answer: ______________________
🎓 PLAN IT · Write ONE more quiz item of your own to test your student
5KEY FRAMES & TEACHER TOOLKIT~4 MIN

The frames of this lesson — say each one aloud twice:

1  I’m going to ___ after class.
2  I’ll probably ___ / I don’t think I’ll ___.
3  What are you doing this weekend?

Teacher phrases — say each aloud once so they are ready:

"Let me show you something interesting…"
"Look at this sentence. What do you notice?"
"Almost! Remember the pattern — try again."
"Now you try one. Make a sentence about YOUR day."
FINAL CHECK BEFORE YOU TEACH:

I can explain each rule with my own example

I have 2 true examples + 1 discovery question ready

I solved the quiz items and wrote one of my own

I know what to say when my student says “I will call you when I will arrive”

Your student’s learning is in your hands. Show before you tell, ask before you explain, and make them produce at least two sentences of their own. Good luck, teacher! 🎓
