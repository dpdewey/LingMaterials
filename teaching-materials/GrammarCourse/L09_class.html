IF I WERE YOU…

Modals II — advice, suggestion, preference, prohibition · 120 min · minimal teacher instruction — content is delivered by the study packets

← L8INDEXL10 →
HOW THIS SESSION WORKS: The teacher facilitates but does not lecture. Grammar content is delivered by the study packets (Phase 2), which the TEACH group (Packet A) and the INDIVIDUAL STUDY group (Packet B) complete separately — different rooms or opposite sides of the room, no cross-talk. If any Phase 1–2 instruction is given, give it to each group separately and identically. All later phases are mixed and student-run from this guide.
1REVIEW RETRIEVAL — student-run10 MIN
  • Pairs quiz each other on the 4 items below (from earlier lessons — spaced practice!). Say the FULL sentence aloud, not just the letter.
  • Then: each student asks the partner one exit-ticket question they wrote last lesson.
1. (L8) She ___ speak three languages.
  a) can   b) cans
2. (L8) You ___ pay — it's free!
  a) don't have to   b) mustn't
3. (L7) I think Peru ___ win tonight.
  a) will   b) is winning
4. (L6) Can you tell me how much it ___?
  a) costs   b) does it cost

answers: 1a 2a 3a 4a

Spacing + interleaving: items reach back several lessons, not just the last one. Teacher talk: ~1 min.
2PACKET STUDY — groups A and B, SEPARATELY25 MIN
  • TEACH group: Packet A (L09_packetA). Study to teach — complete every purple PLAN IT box.
  • INDIVIDUAL STUDY group: Packet B (L09_packetB). Study for yourself — complete every green MY NOTES box.
  • Groups work in separate spaces. The teacher circulates to keep time and answer procedural (not content) questions.
This phase replaces teacher presentation. Packets are content-identical; only the orientation differs.
3TEACH / CONSOLIDATE12 MIN
  • TEACH group: pair up within the group. Partner 1 teaches the full mini-lesson (explain → examples → discovery question → mini-quiz) for 5 minutes; then swap roles for 5 minutes.
  • INDIVIDUAL STUDY group: close the packet. From memory: rewrite the three frames, your two examples, and the answer to the mini-quiz. Then reopen and self-check.
4SORT RACE — mixed pairs (one A + one B)8 MIN
Mark each item: + BASE VERB = A  or  + -ING = B. First pair with all 6 correct wins. Then explain ONE trap item to another pair.
1. Why don’t you ___ (call) her?   
2. How about ___ (order) pizza tonight?   
3. You’d better ___ (leave) now.   
4. Let’s ___ (watch) the match together.   
5. What about ___ (ask) the teacher?   
6. I’d rather ___ (walk) than take the bus.   

answers: 1A 2B 3A 4A 5B 6A

5THE ADVICE COLUMN — problem-cards gap25 MIN
  • Everyone writes one real-ish problem on a card, anonymously (money, friends, study, neighbors… keep it school-appropriate and not too personal). Cards go into a pile and are shuffled.
  • Groups of 3 = advice columnist teams. Each team draws two cards and drafts a Delia-style answer per card: one should, one softer suggestion (Why don’t you… / How about…), one If I were you…, and — where the problem is urgent — one ’d better… or…
  • Answers are read aloud; the problem’s anonymous author secretly rates the advice 1–5 with a show of fingers on the count of three.
  • The team with the highest average rating wins the Delia Cup.
6WOULD-YOU-RATHER TOURNAMENT18 MIN
  • Pairs write three creative Would-you-rather questions (frame 3) — funny but answerable: Would you rather speak every language or play every instrument?
  • Speed rounds: pairs merge into fours; each person answers every question with I’d rather… because… — the because is mandatory and must be at least eight words.
  • Each four sends its single best question to the front. Whole-class finale: hands-vote each finalist question; split votes = follow-up interviews (Why would you rather…?).
  • Champion question wins; its authors set the first review question next lesson.
7ERROR AUCTION — student-run feedback10 MIN
  • During Phases 5–6, each pair writes down two sentences they heard or said (one they think is correct, one they suspect has an error) on slips.
  • Slips go to the front. A volunteer host reads each aloud; teams bet points on CORRECT or FIX IT — a FIX IT bet must include the repair to win.
  • The teacher only referees disputed calls (~2 min of talk).
