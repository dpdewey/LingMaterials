2 · Ask your partner
  1. Do you like to travel?
  2. What city do you want to visit?
  3. Do you like the beach or the mountains?
  4. What is a famous place in your country?
3 · Say it like this

SAY IT LIKE THIS

  • I want to visit ___.
  • I like ___. / I don’t like ___.
  • It is famous for ___.
  • Last year, I went to ___.
  • My favourite place is ___.
