I LOVE FOOTBALL

Agree vs. Disagree Debate · CEFR A2–B1
Talk like this
I agree because… I don't agree because… Maybe, but… I think …

How to play

  1. Read the sentence. One side agrees, the other side disagrees.
  2. Choose 2–3 reasons from your column. You can add your own.
  3. Take turns. Use a frame from the bar above to answer your partner.
  4. After two minutes, swap sides and try the other column.
1Football is fun.

Agree

  • It is exciting to watch.
  • I enjoy playing with friends.
  • It gives people something to talk about.

Disagree

  • It is boring for me.
  • I prefer other sports.
  • The games are too long.
2Winning is the most important thing.

Agree

  • Teams play to win.
  • Winning makes people happy.
  • A good result is important.

Disagree

  • Having fun is more important.
  • You can learn when you lose.
  • Teamwork matters more.
3Football brings people together.

Agree

  • Friends watch games together.
  • Families support teams together.
  • It gives people a common interest.

Disagree

  • People support different teams.
  • Not everyone likes football.
  • It can cause arguments between fans.
4Top players have too much money.

Agree

  • Some earn millions of dollars.
  • Teachers and doctors earn less.
  • No one needs that much money.

Disagree

  • They work very hard.
  • Millions of people watch them.
  • They have special skills.
5It is okay to be sad when your team loses.

Agree

  • Fans care about their teams.
  • Being sad for a short time is normal.
  • It shows you support your team.

Disagree

  • It is only a game.
  • Losing is part of sport.
  • There are more important things in life.
6I like to watch sport with people.

Agree

  • It is more fun with friends.
  • We can talk about the game.
  • We can celebrate together.

Disagree

  • I like to watch quietly.
  • I can focus better alone.
  • Watching alone is more relaxing.
7My country's team makes me happy.

Agree

  • I feel proud of my country.
  • I enjoy supporting my national team.
  • It brings people together.

Disagree

  • I support a club team more.
  • I do not watch international games.
  • I am not interested in sports.
8Sport is good for us.

Agree

  • It helps people stay healthy.
  • It reduces stress.
  • It teaches teamwork.

Disagree

  • Some sports cause injuries.
  • People can be healthy in other ways.
  • Some sports are expensive.

Useful Responses

Agreeing

  • I agree.
  • That's true.
  • I think so too.
  • Good point.

Disagreeing

  • I don't agree.
  • I see it differently.
  • Maybe, but…
  • I'm not sure about that.

Giving reasons

  • Because…
  • For example…
  • In my opinion…
  • I think… because…

For the teacher

Pairs with the I Love Football opinion survey — same eight statements, same order. Put the "Talk like this" bar on the board, or print and cut each card so groups argue one statement at a time. For mixed levels, assign the weaker speaker the Agree column (shorter clauses) first, then swap. To extend: students rank the eight statements from "most true" to "least true" and defend their top choice.
