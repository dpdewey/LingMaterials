🗣️ His, Her, or Their?

📌 Nota gramatical para los estudiantes

His se usa para un hombre — His name is Luis. Her se usa para una mujer — Her name is Ana. Their se usa para dos o más personas — Their names are Luis and Ana. Con "their", la palabra "name" también cambia a "names".

What time is it, Mr. Fox? Numbers practice — very active!

Line trainees up against a wall. One person is "Mr. Fox" with their back turned. Everyone asks together: "What time is it, Mr. Fox?" Tap New Answer — if it's a number, take that many steps forward. If it's MIDNIGHT, Mr. Fox turns and chases everyone back to the wall!

Formen una fila contra la pared. Una persona es el "Sr. Zorro" de espaldas. Todos preguntan juntos: "What time is it, Mr. Fox?" Si sale un número, den esa cantidad de pasos. Si sale "MIDNIGHT" (medianoche), ¡el zorro persigue a todos de regreso a la pared!
