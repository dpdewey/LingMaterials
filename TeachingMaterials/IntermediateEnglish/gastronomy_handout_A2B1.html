Food I Like · CEFR A2–B1 · 65 min

2 · Ask your partner
  1. What is your favourite food?
  2. What do you eat in the morning?
  3. Can you cook? What can you cook?
  4. Do you like fruit? Which fruit?
3 · Say it like this

SAY IT LIKE THIS

  • My favourite food is ___.
  • I like ___. / I don’t like ___.
  • In the morning, I eat ___.
  • It is sweet / spicy / good.
  • I can cook ___.
4 · Different people

Is home food better than restaurant food?

A MOTHERHome food is good and cheap. I cook for my family.
A STUDENTI like restaurants. The food is fast and fun.
A COOKI make new food in my restaurant. I love my job.
A FARMERI grow good food. Fresh food is the best.
5 · Agree or disagree?

Agree, not sure, or disagree? Say one reason.

Home food is better than restaurant food.
Fast food is bad for us.
Everyone can learn to cook.
Breakfast is the most important meal.
The food in my country is very good.
6 · Your turn (choose one)

Choose ONE. Use short sentences.

  • Write 4 foods you like.
  • Draw your favourite meal and write its name.
  • Make a small menu for a café: 3 foods and 2 drinks.
7 · One word

Say one food you like.
