TEACH FOR SUCCESS! · LESSON PLAN (teacher)

I LOVE FOOTBALL

I Love Football · CEFR A2–B1 · 65 min

Strong A2 / emerging B165 min · 55-min trimTeacher talk < 1/3

Why this lesson

Many people love football. When their team wins, they are very happy. Football brings people together.

MY NOTE — personal reminders and in-the-moment cuesThis is the A2–B1 edition. Sentences are short and the grammar is simple. It’s anticipated that students need more time and more examples here, so model every task yourself first, then let them copy the pattern.
1Warm-up: look at the survey5 minTTT low

Give out the survey. Students read the short sentences and tick agree, not sure, or disagree. No talking yet. This shows them the topic and the new words.

MY NOTE — personal reminders and in-the-moment cuesIt’s likely a few words are new. Say: ‘Don’t worry, we learn them next.’
2New words (two sets)12 minTTT med

Set 1:

MY NOTE — personal reminders and in-the-moment cuesShow a picture or do an action for each word. Say it 3 times together as a class. Students will likely know one or two already — that builds confidence.
3Watch / listen7 minTTT low

A short clip of fans happy after a goal, or people watching a game together. Play it two times. After, students say ONE word they saw.

No video? Read this aloud

Read aloud, slowly: “The team plays. A player kicks the ball. Goal! The fans are very happy. They sing together.”

4Talk with a partner10 minTTT low

Pairs ask and answer the questions. The frames below help them make full sentences.

  1. Do you like football?
  2. What is your favourite team?
  3. Do you play a sport? Which sport?
  4. How do you feel when your team wins?

SAY IT LIKE THIS

  • My favourite team is ___.
  • I like ___. / I don’t like ___.
  • I feel happy when ___.
  • I play ___ on ___ (day).
  • I watch football with ___.
MY NOTE — personal reminders and in-the-moment cuesIt’s anticipated that some pairs finish fast. Ask them: ‘Why?’ to get one more sentence.
5Language Clinic 15 minTTT med

Anticipated focus: present simple (I play. He plays. They win.). It’s likely students will drop the ‘s’ (he play → he plays).

Write 2–3 student sentences on the board (no names). Fix them together, slowly.

6Different people say different things9 minTTT low

The question: Is winning important?

Give each student one card. They read it and say that person’s idea in their own words.

A PLAYERI want to win. Winning is good.
A FANI like to watch with friends. Winning is not everything.
A COACHI want my team to play well and have fun.
A CHILDI play because it is fun!
7Agree or disagree?8 minTTT low

Read each sentence. Students stand on one side for AGREE, the other for DISAGREE, the middle for NOT SURE. Then one student says why.

Football is fun.
Winning is the most important thing.
Football brings people together.
Top players have too much money.
It is okay to be sad when your team loses.
8Language Clinic 24 minTTT med

Anticipated focus: feeling words (happy, sad, excited, tired). Students will probably need help choosing the right one.

9Your turn (choose one)5 minTTT low

Choose ONE. Use short sentences.

  • Write your team’s name and 2 colours.
  • Draw your favourite player and write the name.
  • Make a poster: a team name and one word.
MY NOTE — personal reminders and in-the-moment cuesFor the 55-minute trim: give this as homework and end after ‘Agree or disagree?’
10One word to finish2 minTTT low

Go around the class: Say one word about football. Just one word each.
