TOURNAMENT OF CHAMPIONS II

Grand interleaved review — the whole course · 120 min · minimal teacher instruction — content is delivered by the study packets

← L23INDEX
HOW THIS SESSION WORKS: The teacher facilitates but does not lecture. Grammar content is delivered by the study packets (Phase 2), which the TEACH group (Packet A) and the INDIVIDUAL STUDY group (Packet B) complete separately — different rooms or opposite sides of the room, no cross-talk. If any Phase 1–2 instruction is given, give it to each group separately and identically. All later phases are mixed and student-run from this guide.
1REVIEW RETRIEVAL — student-run10 MIN
  • Pairs quiz each other on the 4 items below (from earlier lessons — spaced practice!). Say the FULL sentence aloud, not just the letter.
  • Then: each student asks the partner one exit-ticket question they wrote last lesson.
1. (L23) The train leaves ___ 9:40.
  a) at   b) in
2. (L23) She was born ___ 1999.
  a) in   b) at
3. (L22) Although it rained, ___ we went.
  a) — (nothing)   b) but
4. (L21) The accident ___ at noon.
  a) happened   b) was happened

answers: 1a 2a 3a 4a

Spacing + interleaving: items reach back several lessons, not just the last one. Teacher talk: ~1 min.
2PACKET STUDY — groups A and B, SEPARATELY25 MIN
  • TEACH group: Packet A (L24_packetA). Study to teach — complete every purple PLAN IT box.
  • INDIVIDUAL STUDY group: Packet B (L24_packetB). Study for yourself — complete every green MY NOTES box.
  • Groups work in separate spaces. The teacher circulates to keep time and answer procedural (not content) questions.
This phase replaces teacher presentation. Packets are content-identical; only the orientation differs.
3TEACH / CONSOLIDATE12 MIN
  • TEACH group: pair up within the group. Partner 1 teaches the full mini-lesson (explain → examples → discovery question → mini-quiz) for 5 minutes; then swap roles for 5 minutes.
  • INDIVIDUAL STUDY group: close the packet. From memory: rewrite the three frames, your two examples, and the answer to the mini-quiz. Then reopen and self-check.
4SORT RACE — mixed pairs (one A + one B)8 MIN
Mark each item: CORRECT = A  or  A TRAP SURVIVED! = B. First pair with all 6 correct wins. Then explain ONE trap item to another pair.
1. I’ve worked here since 2023.   
2. She’s good at solving problems that nobody else wants.   
3. If it will be sunny, we’ll hike.   
4. The informations you sent was helpful.   
5. Could you tell me where the exit is?   
6. He stopped to smoke last year — no more cigarettes ever. (meaning!)   

answers: 1A 2A 3B 4B 5A 6B

5THE GRAND STATION TOURNAMENT — all territories35 MIN
  • Seven stations, one per territory (see the map), hosted by today’s TEACH-group students — each armed by Packet A to run one territory with a lightning quiz, a trap card from the Seven, and a production dare.
  • Visiting trios rotate every 5 minutes. New championship rule: at each station, ONE answer must be justified aloud with a rule (It’s ‘were’ because unreal if-clauses use were for all persons) — hosts award a justification bonus.
  • Hosts track a Trap Leaderboard: which of the Seven Deadly Traps caught the most victims today?
  • Totals announced; the most-feared trap is written giant on the board — and ceremonially crossed out by the whole class.
6THE FINAL AUCTION & TIME CAPSULE25 MIN
  • The last Error Auction, all-course edition: sentence slips harvested from today plus classics the teacher saved all term. Teams bet their tournament points; FIX IT claims must name the territory and produce the repair.
  • The final lot is the Thief’s masterpiece — one sentence hiding THREE errors from three territories. Full pot only for all three.
  • Time capsule: every student writes two sentences on a card — the error they used to make (crossed out, gloriously) and the correct version they own now — plus one line of advice for next semester’s students.
  • Cards are read aloud in pairs, then sealed in the class envelope for the next cohort. Applause. You’ve earned every clause of it.
7ERROR AUCTION — student-run feedback10 MIN
  • During Phases 5–6, each pair writes down two sentences they heard or said (one they think is correct, one they suspect has an error) on slips.
  • Slips go to the front. A volunteer host reads each aloud; teams bet points on CORRECT or FIX IT — a FIX IT bet must include the repair to win.
  • The teacher only referees disputed calls (~2 min of talk).
8EXIT TICKET & HOMEWORK LAUNCH10 MIN
From memory — no notes:

1. The trap you’ve officially retired (crossed out) + its fix:

2. Your English goal for the next 12 weeks, in one conditional:

3. One sentence of advice for the next cohort — make it worthy of the envelope:

  • Open the L24 Practice Module (~2 hours, in parts). Do parts A–F before next class; save COME BACK for two days later.
  • Don’t skip the SHARE task — you will read your report to a partner at the start of the next lesson.
TIME TOTAL: ~120 min · Teacher talk: under 15 min · Everything else is pair/group interaction.
