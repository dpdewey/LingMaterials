YOUR MISSION: In a few minutes, you will be the teacher. A classmate will learn this from YOU — your explanation, your examples, your questions. As you study, keep asking: How will I make my student understand this? The purple boxes are your teaching plan.
1THE MAP OF EVERYTHING SO FAR~6 MIN
Eleven lessons, five territories: Past (past vs. progressive; used to/would) · Perfect (for/since; ever/just/yet) · Questions (do-support; embedded & tags) · Future & Modals (will/going to; can/must/should) · Nouns (much/many; a/the/zero). Today nothing is new — everything is mixed.
The exam trick: first ask WHICH territory a sentence lives in, then apply its rule.
🎓 PLAN IT · How will you explain this?

Write the words you will actually SAY to your student. Tip: draw or show an example first — teachers who show are remembered!

2WHY MIXING BEATS BLOCKS~6 MIN
Interleaved practice feels harder and messier than reviewing one topic at a time — and that difficulty is exactly what builds durable learning. Expect to hesitate; hesitation is retrieval working.
Blocked: 10 article items in a row → easy, forgettable.
Interleaved: article, then modal, then tag question → hard, sticky.

3THE FIVE CLASSIC TRAPS TO RE-ARM AGAINST~5 MIN
1) was knowing — statives stay simple. 2) I live here since 2019 — duration needs perfect. 3) Where you did buy… — QUASM order. 4) must to / should to — modal + bare verb. 5) The life is hard / informations — zero article & noncount.

4MINI-QUIZ — test your student!~4 MIN

Solve them yourself FIRST and note the answers small — then give them to your student at the end and check their learning.

Q1 · Fix all errors: “She cans speak French since three years.”  my answer: ______________________
Q2 · Which territory (past/perfect/questions/modals/nouns) does this error belong to: “How many money do you need?”  my answer: ______________________
🎓 PLAN IT · Write ONE more quiz item of your own to test your student
5KEY FRAMES & TEACHER TOOLKIT~4 MIN

The frames of this lesson — say each one aloud twice:

1  Are you sure? Why is it ___ and not ___?
2  It depends on whether ___ .
3  Let me explain it with an example: ___ .

Teacher phrases — say each aloud once so they are ready:

"Let me show you something interesting…"
"Look at this sentence. What do you notice?"
"Almost! Remember the pattern — try again."
"Now you try one. Make a sentence about YOUR day."
FINAL CHECK BEFORE YOU TEACH:

I can explain each rule with my own example

I have 2 true examples + 1 discovery question ready

I solved the quiz items and wrote one of my own

I know what to say when my student says “I have seen him yesterday”

Your student’s learning is in your hands. Show before you tell, ask before you explain, and make them produce at least two sentences of their own. Good luck, teacher! 🎓
