COURSE INDEX

B1 intermediate · mixed L1s · packet-delivered content · A/B peer-teaching design

HOW TO USE THIS COURSE: Each lesson = 4 files. The CLASS GUIDE runs the 120-minute session with minimal teacher instruction: content is delivered by the study packets, which the TEACH group (PACKET A — study to teach a peer) and the INDIVIDUAL STUDY group (PACKET B — parallel independent study) complete separately. The PRACTICE module is ~2 hours of interactive homework with instant feedback (parts A–F now, COME BACK two days later). Lessons 12 and 24 are interleaved review tournaments. Print packets and class guides; the practice module runs in any browser.
