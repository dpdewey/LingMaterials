HAVE YOU EVER…?

Present perfect II — experience & recent news · 120 min · minimal teacher instruction — content is delivered by the study packets

← L3INDEXL5 →
HOW THIS SESSION WORKS: The teacher facilitates but does not lecture. Grammar content is delivered by the study packets (Phase 2), which the TEACH group (Packet A) and the INDIVIDUAL STUDY group (Packet B) complete separately — different rooms or opposite sides of the room, no cross-talk. If any Phase 1–2 instruction is given, give it to each group separately and identically. All later phases are mixed and student-run from this guide.
1REVIEW RETRIEVAL — student-run10 MIN
  • Pairs quiz each other on the 4 items below (from earlier lessons — spaced practice!). Say the FULL sentence aloud, not just the letter.
  • Then: each student asks the partner one exit-ticket question they wrote last lesson.
1. (L3) I've been here ___ two hours.
  a) for   b) since
2. (L3) We ___ friends for ten years.
  a) have been   b) are
3. (L2) He didn't ___ to drink coffee.
  a) use   b) used
4. (L1) The dog barked while I ___.
  a) was sleeping   b) slept

answers: 1a 2a 3a 4a

Spacing + interleaving: items reach back several lessons, not just the last one. Teacher talk: ~1 min.
2PACKET STUDY — groups A and B, SEPARATELY25 MIN
  • TEACH group: Packet A (L04_packetA). Study to teach — complete every purple PLAN IT box.
  • INDIVIDUAL STUDY group: Packet B (L04_packetB). Study for yourself — complete every green MY NOTES box.
  • Groups work in separate spaces. The teacher circulates to keep time and answer procedural (not content) questions.
This phase replaces teacher presentation. Packets are content-identical; only the orientation differs.
3TEACH / CONSOLIDATE12 MIN
  • TEACH group: pair up within the group. Partner 1 teaches the full mini-lesson (explain → examples → discovery question → mini-quiz) for 5 minutes; then swap roles for 5 minutes.
  • INDIVIDUAL STUDY group: close the packet. From memory: rewrite the three frames, your two examples, and the answer to the mini-quiz. Then reopen and self-check.
4SORT RACE — mixed pairs (one A + one B)8 MIN
Mark each item: PRESENT PERFECT = A  or  SIMPLE PAST = B. First pair with all 6 correct wins. Then explain ONE trap item to another pair.
1. I have ___ (see) snow only once in my life.   
2. We ___ (visit) my grandparents last weekend.   
3. She has just ___ (finish) her exam.   
4. They ___ (get) married in 2018.   
5. Have you ___ (be) to Mexico? (ever)   
6. I ___ (lose) my wallet yesterday.   

answers: 1A 2B 3A 4B 5A 6B

5FIND SOMEONE WHO HAS… — mingle bingo22 MIN
  • Board bingo: class brainstorms 9 experiences into a 3×3 grid (has ridden a horse, has met someone famous, has broken a bone, has cooked for 10+ people…).
  • Mingle (12 min): ask ONLY frame 1 questions. A YES must be followed by TWO past-tense detail questions (When did you…? Where was it?) — this drills the perfect→past switch.
  • Write the person’s name + one detail in the square. Three in a row = BINGO, but keep going for a full house.
  • Report (5 min): the best discovery per student: Amina has met a famous singer — she saw him at the airport in Dubai!
6THE ALREADY/YET RACE — roommate chores gap18 MIN
  • Pairs are “roommates” preparing a surprise party in 30 minutes. Each gets a different half-completed to-do list of 8 tasks (A knows tasks 1–4 status; B knows 5–8).
  • Phone-call style, back-to-back: check every task — Have you bought the cake yet? — Yes, I’ve already bought it! / No, not yet!
  • Agree who does each unfinished task; note it. First pair with a complete plan wins.
  • Twist round: teacher calls “guests have just arrived!” — pairs report their status in 30 seconds using just/already/yet.
7ERROR AUCTION — student-run feedback10 MIN
  • During Phases 5–6, each pair writes down two sentences they heard or said (one they think is correct, one they suspect has an error) on slips.
  • Slips go to the front. A volunteer host reads each aloud; teams bet points on CORRECT or FIX IT — a FIX IT bet must include the repair to win.
  • The teacher only referees disputed calls (~2 min of talk).
