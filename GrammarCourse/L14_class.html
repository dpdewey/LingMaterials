TEACH FOR SUCCESS! · LESSON 14 OF 24 · CLASS SESSION GUIDE

STOP TO THINK, STOP THINKING

Gerunds & infinitives II — meaning changes & prepositions · 120 min · minimal teacher instruction — content is delivered by the study packets

← L13INDEXL15 →
HOW THIS SESSION WORKS: The teacher facilitates but does not lecture. Grammar content is delivered by the study packets (Phase 2), which the TEACH group (Packet A) and the INDIVIDUAL STUDY group (Packet B) complete separately — different rooms or opposite sides of the room, no cross-talk. If any Phase 1–2 instruction is given, give it to each group separately and identically. All later phases are mixed and student-run from this guide.
1REVIEW RETRIEVAL — student-run10 MIN
  • Pairs quiz each other on the 4 items below (from earlier lessons — spaced practice!). Say the FULL sentence aloud, not just the letter.
  • Then: each student asks the partner one exit-ticket question they wrote last lesson.
1. (L13) I enjoy ___ old movies.
  a) watching   b) to watch
2. (L13) He finished ___ at noon.
  a) working   b) to work
3. (L12) You ___ pay — it's free.
  a) don't have to   b) mustn't
4. (L11) It's ___ best café in town.
  a) the   b) a

answers: 1a 2a 3a 4a

Spacing + interleaving: items reach back several lessons, not just the last one. Teacher talk: ~1 min.
2PACKET STUDY — groups A and B, SEPARATELY25 MIN
  • TEACH group: Packet A (L14_packetA). Study to teach — complete every purple PLAN IT box.
  • INDIVIDUAL STUDY group: Packet B (L14_packetB). Study for yourself — complete every green MY NOTES box.
  • Groups work in separate spaces. The teacher circulates to keep time and answer procedural (not content) questions.
This phase replaces teacher presentation. Packets are content-identical; only the orientation differs.
3TEACH / CONSOLIDATE12 MIN
  • TEACH group: pair up within the group. Partner 1 teaches the full mini-lesson (explain → examples → discovery question → mini-quiz) for 5 minutes; then swap roles for 5 minutes.
  • INDIVIDUAL STUDY group: close the packet. From memory: rewrite the three frames, your two examples, and the answer to the mini-quiz. Then reopen and self-check.
4SORT RACE — mixed pairs (one A + one B)8 MIN
Mark each item: LOOKS BACK (GERUND) = A  or  LOOKS FORWARD (TO + VERB) = B. First pair with all 6 correct wins. Then explain ONE trap item to another pair.
1. I remember ___ (meet) her at that party in 2019.   
2. Please remember ___ (buy) bread on your way home.   
3. He stopped ___ (eat) sugar last year — completely.   
4. We stopped ___ (take) photos of the volcano, then drove on.   
5. I’ll never forget ___ (see) the ocean for the first time.   
6. Don’t forget ___ (turn) off the stove!   

answers: 1A 2B 3A 4B 5A 6B

5TWO-MEANINGS THEATER22 MIN
  • Pairs draw a meaning-changer card: stop smoking / stop to smoke · remember locking / remember to lock · try turning it off / try to lift the box · forget paying / forget to pay.
  • Each pair prepares TWO ten-second silent mini-scenes — one per meaning — and one spoken caption sentence per scene.
  • Perform both scenes in random order; the class must call out which caption fits which scene and say WHY: Scene two is ‘stopped to smoke’ — he paused the walk in order to smoke!
  • Vote for the clearest performance and the funniest. Encore round: the winning pair performs a NEW pair of meanings improvised from the class’s shout-out.
6THE TALENT AGENCY — preposition-chunk interviews20 MIN
  • Half the class = talent agents, half = candidates. Candidates prepare a profile using ONLY preposition chunks: three good at -ing, one bad at -ing (honesty sells!), one interested in -ing, one excited about -ing.
  • Agents interview three candidates each (What are you best at? What are you tired of doing in your current job?) and take notes — they must sign ONE client.
  • Agents pitch their chosen client to the class in three sentences: Sign Marisol: she’s brilliant at calming angry customers, she’s interested in learning Portuguese, and she’s only bad at arriving on time.
  • Candidates confirm or correct the pitch from memory — accuracy check built in. Swap roles if time allows.
7ERROR AUCTION — student-run feedback10 MIN
  • During Phases 5–6, each pair writes down two sentences they heard or said (one they think is correct, one they suspect has an error) on slips.
  • Slips go to the front. A volunteer host reads each aloud; teams bet points on CORRECT or FIX IT — a FIX IT bet must include the repair to win.
  • The teacher only referees disputed calls (~2 min of talk).
8EXIT TICKET & HOMEWORK LAUNCH10 MIN
From memory — no notes:

1. One stopped + -ing truth about you:

2. One remember to task for tomorrow:

3. Your one-line talent-agency profile (good at + -ing):

  • Open the L14 Practice Module (~2 hours, in parts). Do parts A–F before next class; save COME BACK for two days later.
  • Don’t skip the SHARE task — you will read your report to a partner at the start of the next lesson.
TIME TOTAL: ~120 min · Teacher talk: under 15 min · Everything else is pair/group interaction.
