HOW THIS SESSION WORKS: The teacher facilitates but does not lecture. Grammar content is delivered by the study packets (Phase 2), which the TEACH group (Packet A) and the INDIVIDUAL STUDY group (Packet B) complete separately — different rooms or opposite sides of the room, no cross-talk. If any Phase 1–2 instruction is given, give it to each group separately and identically. All later phases are mixed and student-run from this guide.
1REVIEW RETRIEVAL — student-run10 MIN
  • Pairs quiz each other on the 4 items below (from earlier lessons — spaced practice!). Say the FULL sentence aloud, not just the letter.
  • Then: each student asks the partner one exit-ticket question they wrote last lesson.
1. (L7) The phone's ringing — I ___ answer!
  a) 'll   b) 'm going to
2. (L7) We ___ dinner with Ana on Friday. (arranged)
  a) 're having   b) will have
3. (L6) I wonder what time it ___.
  a) starts   b) does start
4. (L5) What ___ ? Tell me everything!
  a) happened   b) did happen

answers: 1a 2a 3a 4a

Spacing + interleaving: items reach back several lessons, not just the last one. Teacher talk: ~1 min.
2PACKET STUDY — groups A and B, SEPARATELY25 MIN
  • TEACH group: Packet A (L08_packetA). Study to teach — complete every purple PLAN IT box.
  • INDIVIDUAL STUDY group: Packet B (L08_packetB). Study for yourself — complete every green MY NOTES box.
  • Groups work in separate spaces. The teacher circulates to keep time and answer procedural (not content) questions.
This phase replaces teacher presentation. Packets are content-identical; only the orientation differs.
3TEACH / CONSOLIDATE12 MIN
  • TEACH group: pair up within the group. Partner 1 teaches the full mini-lesson (explain → examples → discovery question → mini-quiz) for 5 minutes; then swap roles for 5 minutes.
  • INDIVIDUAL STUDY group: close the packet. From memory: rewrite the three frames, your two examples, and the answer to the mini-quiz. Then reopen and self-check.
4SORT RACE — mixed pairs (one A + one B)8 MIN
Mark each item: NOT NECESSARY = A  or  PROHIBITED = B. First pair with all 6 correct wins. Then explain ONE trap item to another pair.
1. You ___ wear a tie here — it’s casual. (don’t have to / mustn’t?)   
2. You ___ park here — it’s a fire exit.   
3. Students ___ bring a dictionary; the school provides them.   
4. You ___ feed the animals at the zoo.   
5. You ___ print the ticket — your phone is fine.   
6. Passengers ___ smoke on the plane.   

answers: 1A 2B 3A 4B 5A 6B

5THE MYSTERY BAG — deduction lab20 MIN
  • Each group of 4 receives (or imagines from the teacher’s list) a sealed bag/box with a mystery object. They may shake, weigh, smell — not open.
  • Five deduction sentences per group, graded by certainty: two with might/could, two with must + a reason, one with can’t + a reason (It can’t be a phone — it’s too light).
  • Groups visit each other’s bags and add one deduction to each. All sentences on the board.
  • The reveal! Award points: 3 for a correct must, 1 for a correct might, minus 1 for a wrong can’t. Deduction champions announced.
6RULES OF THE LAND — visitor briefing gap22 MIN
  • Pairs. Each student secretly picks a place they know well (their job, a national park, their grandmother’s kitchen, an online game server) and writes 6 rules: two must/have to, two can’t/mustn’t, two don’t have to.
  • Briefing: partner is a new visitor and hears the rules WITHOUT the place being named — then gets 5 yes/no questions (Can I eat there? Do I have to pay?) and guesses the place.
  • Swap. Then re-pair and report your first partner’s strangest rule: In Rosa’s kitchen, you mustn’t touch the blue pot. Ever.
  • Close: class votes on the strictest place of the day.
7ERROR AUCTION — student-run feedback10 MIN
  • During Phases 5–6, each pair writes down two sentences they heard or said (one they think is correct, one they suspect has an error) on slips.
  • Slips go to the front. A volunteer host reads each aloud; teams bet points on CORRECT or FIX IT — a FIX IT bet must include the repair to win.
  • The teacher only referees disputed calls (~2 min of talk).
