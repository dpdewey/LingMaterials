Tips

  • Name your proficiency precisely on the resume: CEFR level (B2, C1), ACTFL OPI rating, or ILR level.
  • Pair the language with a second skill employers search for: data, writing and editing, business, teaching licensure, or a security clearance path.
  • Many of the best jobs never post to aggregators: check the Search-by-hand list (federal careers, UN and EU, Goethe-Institut, DAAD, American Councils).
  • A semester abroad, an internship, or translation samples count as experience; list them as such.
